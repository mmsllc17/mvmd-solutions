About

Built on culture.
Delivered with discipline.

I'm Marv. I grew up in the culture — the kind you find on New York City streets, in the music, the sport, the way people move through this city and make things out of nothing. That's always been my reference point as a designer.

Over eight years I've taken that sensibility into boardrooms, brand strategies, and creative systems for organizations across New York — from financial exchanges in Lower Manhattan to community cycling clubs in the outer boroughs. My work sits at the intersection of creative direction, visual design, and the operational discipline to actually ship.
